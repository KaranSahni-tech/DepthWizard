SIH PS 175 Monocular Depth & Surface Extraction Engine

DepthWizard uses state-of-the-art zero-shot monocular depth estimation powered by PyTorch and Apple Depth Pro. It generates high-resolution canonical depth predictions and extracts relative Digital Surface Models (rDSM), building height profiles, terrain relief, and edge contours from single RGB remote-sensing imagery.

Overview of the 12 thematic elevation and surface outputs

DepthWizard transforms single-view aerial RGB images into 12 multi-layer GIS outputs without requiring stereo pairs or expensive LiDAR hardware. For support or inquiries regarding SIH PS 175 presentation guidelines, consult the embedded metadata and export packages.
